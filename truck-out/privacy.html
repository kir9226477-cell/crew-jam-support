Privacy Policy

Truck Out: Build a City · last updated November 23, 2026

No data collected

Truck Out does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use the network to play.

Data on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: finished levels, your boosters and the sound and vibration settings. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version of Truck Out starts collecting any data, for example to show ads, this page will be updated before that version is released.
