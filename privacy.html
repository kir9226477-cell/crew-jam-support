Privacy Policy

Crew Jam · last updated November 16, 2026

No data collected

Crew Jam does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use the network to play.

Data on your device

The game saves which levels you have finished on your device only, so your town is still there next time. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version of Crew Jam starts collecting any data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
