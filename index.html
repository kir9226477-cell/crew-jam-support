Crew Jam

A sorting puzzle on a construction site

Support

Found a bug, stuck on a level or have an idea? Write to ruslankir317@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number.

How to play

Tap a worker to send them to the bus of their crew. A worker can move only if the path to the top row is free. If their bus is not at a stop yet, they wait on a WAIT spot. A full bus drives to the site and the house grows.

The concrete mixer rides in the bus queue. When its turn comes, it drives onto the site and waits a few moves for any Concrete bus. Get the concrete crew there before the counter hits zero.
